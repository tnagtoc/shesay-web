What is She Say?

She Say (她说) is a community app built for women to share their lives and discoveries. It is a warm and safe space where you can record your everyday moments, document what you have seen and learned, and meet people who share your interests.

  • Post daily moments — share the joy of everyday life;
  • Publish reports — record what you've seen and learned;
  • Join Tea Rooms — find like-minded people and chat about the topics you love.

We are committed to keeping She Say a friendly and secure community, with content moderation and reporting tools in place to protect every user.
